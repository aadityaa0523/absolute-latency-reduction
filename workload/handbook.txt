Riverside Institute of Technology. Student Regulations Handbook, version 3.

This handbook is the single source of truth for student rules at Riverside Institute of Technology. Where an academic department publishes a stricter rule, the stricter rule applies to that department only. Every clause below is numbered so that it can be cited in grievances and appeals.

Clause 1. Attendance. Every student must attend at least 75 percent of the classes held in each course. A student whose attendance in a course falls below 65 percent is barred from the end-semester examination of that course. Medical leave supported by a doctor's certificate counts as present for up to 10 days in a semester, provided the certificate reaches the department office within 7 days of the student's return.

Clause 2. Library loans. An undergraduate student may borrow at most 4 books at a time, each for 14 days. A loan can be renewed twice, unless another student has reserved the book. The late fee is 2 rupees per day for each overdue book. A book that is lost must be replaced or paid for at its current market price plus a handling charge of 100 rupees.

Clause 3. Hostel timings. Hostel gates close at 10:30 pm from Sunday to Thursday, and at 11:30 pm on Friday and Saturday. A student who enters late three times in one term receives a written warning that is copied to the guardian. Overnight leave requires the warden's signature at least one day in advance.

Clause 4. Examination structure. Each course carries 100 marks: 40 marks for internal assessment and 60 marks for the end-semester examination. To pass a course a student needs at least 40 marks in total and at least 24 marks in the end-semester examination. A student who fails may take the supplementary examination held in the following July.

Clause 5. Re-evaluation. A student may apply for re-evaluation of an end-semester answer script within 5 working days of the result being published. The fee is 500 rupees per paper, refunded in full if the marks change by 10 or more. The outcome is announced within 15 days of the application.

Clause 6. Grievance committee. The student grievance committee meets on the first Tuesday of every month. A complaint submitted through the student portal is acknowledged within 48 hours. The committee must give a written decision within 21 days of the meeting at which the complaint is heard.

Clause 7. Laboratory safety. Closed footwear is mandatory in all laboratories, and food and drink are not allowed. Any incident, however small, must be reported to the laboratory supervisor within 1 hour. Students may not work alone in a laboratory after 6 pm.

Clause 8. Merit scholarship. The top 5 percent of students in each branch receive a merit scholarship that waives 50 percent of the tuition fee. The scholarship is renewed each year only if the student's cumulative grade point average stays at or above 8.5 and there is no disciplinary record.

Clause 9. Fee payment. Tuition fees are due on 15 August and 15 January. A late fee of 100 rupees per week applies for up to 4 weeks. After 4 weeks without payment, course registration is blocked until the dues and all late fees are cleared.

Clause 10. Internships. A student may undertake an internship of at most 2 months during the term break. The placement cell must approve the internship at least 10 days before it starts. Any stipend received must be reported to the placement cell, and the internship report is due within 15 days of its end.

Clause 11. Anti-ragging. Ragging in any form is prohibited. A complaint can be made to the anti-ragging squad, whose 24-hour helpline number is printed on every student identity card. A proven case leads to suspension of at least one semester.

Clause 12. Identity cards. Every student must carry the identity card on campus and show it at the gate and in the library. A lost card must be reported within 2 days, and a duplicate costs 150 rupees.

Clause 13. Vehicles. Students may bring two-wheelers to campus only after registering them with the security office. Four-wheelers are not permitted for undergraduate students. The speed limit on campus roads is 20 kilometres per hour.

Clause 14. Guest lectures. Student clubs may invite outside speakers with the written approval of the dean of student affairs, requested at least 7 days before the event. Any event that needs a hall booking must also be entered in the events calendar.

Clause 15. Placement rules. A student who accepts a job offer through the placement cell may not sit for other placement drives, except for a drive whose offered salary is at least 1.5 times the accepted one. Placement registration closes at the end of the sixth semester.

Clause 16. Academic integrity. Copying, impersonation and use of unauthorised material in an examination are treated as malpractice. The first proven offence leads to a zero in that course and a warning. A second offence leads to suspension for one semester.

Clause 17. Health centre. The campus health centre is open from 8 am to 8 pm every day. Emergency cases are taken to the nearest partner hospital, and the institute pays the first 5,000 rupees of emergency treatment for any enrolled student.

Clause 18. Change of branch. A change of branch is allowed only at the end of the first year. It requires a cumulative grade point average of at least 9.0 and a vacant seat in the requested branch. Only one change of branch is allowed during the entire degree.

Clause 19. Leave of absence. A student may take a leave of absence of up to one year for medical or family reasons with the permission of the dean of academics. The time on leave does not count towards the maximum duration of the degree, which is otherwise 6 years.

Clause 20. Amendments. The academic council may amend this handbook at the start of any academic year. Amendments are published on the student portal at least 30 days before they take effect.
